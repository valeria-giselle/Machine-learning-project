# Machine Learning Project – Customer Churn & Segmentation Model Fitness Gym Chain

## 📌 Project Overview
The purpose of this project is to analyze customer behavior in a gym chain and identify the key factors that influence customer churn. Using machine learning techniques, the project aims to predict churn risk and segment users into meaningful groups in order to support data-driven marketing and retention strategies.

This analysis combines exploratory data analysis, supervised learning, and clustering techniques to transform raw data into actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify behavioral and demographic patterns associated with customer cancellation.
- Build a predictive model to estimate churn probability.
- Segment customers into clusters based on usage and engagement.
- Provide strategic recommendations to improve customer retention and loyalty.

- Distribution analysis using histograms

- Supervised learning model to predict customer cancellation

### 4️⃣ Customer Segmentation
- Hierarchical clustering (dendrogram)
- K-Means clustering
- Analysis of cluster characteristics
- Churn rate comparison across clusters

## 📊 Key Insights
Customer Relationship Insights
- Emotional and social connections are stronger predictors of retention than physical proximity.
- Consistent engagement (visits, classes, interactions) leads to higher loyalty.
- Short-term contracts require immediate emotional engagement to reduce churn risk.
- Customers with social ties (friends or partners) are less likely to cancel.

## 🧠 Strategic Marketing Recommendations
### 1️⃣ Create Early Engagement Rituals
Action: Design a “First 30 Days” onboarding experience (e.g., My First Class, Personal Challenge).

Impact: Builds emotional attachment and a sense of belonging from the start.

### 2️⃣ Encourage Social Connections
Action: Promote duo sign-ups, small group classes, or referral rewards.

Impact: Strengthens emotional bonds and reduces anonymity.

### 3️⃣ Segment Marketing Actions by Cluster
Action: Customize communication for high-engagement users, moderate users, and churn-prone users.

Impact: Increases marketing effectiveness through personalization.

### 4️⃣ Celebrate Consistency
Action: Recognize regular attendance and milestones with symbolic rewards (badges, internal awards).

Impact: Reinforces habits and fosters community pride.
